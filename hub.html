SSE Live
1 Super Admin Panel Launch

• Tap Add Rest. dialog.
• Fill Name, Address, Owner details.
• Test "Owner will manage" checkbox (greys out manager).
• Select plan: Free trial (15d), 3m, 6m, 12m.
• Click Generate Pass & save.
• Open View dialog: test Subscription Cancelled checkbox & Manage Subscription.

2 Restaurant Admin App Launch

• Login with credentials generated in Super Admin.
• Print / Generate Table QR codes.
• Print Restaurant-Wide Online QR.
• Add / Remove Waiters with Hierarchy Ranks.
• View Cash Collected & Kitchen status.

3 Customer Mobile Portal Launch

• Scan Table 04 or Online mode.
• Add Karahi, Dum Biryani, Lassi to cart.
• Place order → Sent to Kitchen & Auto-assigned!

4 Waiter Floor App Launch

• Login as Hamza (Rank 1 Lead).
• View auto-assigned order.
• Support for multiple concurrent orders.
• Receives food ready alert bell from kitchen.

5 Kitchen Display (KDS) Launch

• Chef views incoming order with Assigned Waiter Tag.
• Tap Start Prep → Plated & Ready (alerts Waiter App).

6 Cashier Mobile POS Launch

• Tap Table 04 → Bill drawer opens.
• Calculate Cash tendered & change due.
• Print thermal receipt & Settle & Vacate Table.
